**The approximating FEEC theorem.** For an approximating FEEC discretization `F` of the Hodge wave equation of `H` and an orbit-consistent core `Z`: the forward-time reading `F.toDissipFEEC` satisfies the dissipative FEEC theorem with the core `Z.toDissip`, and the conclusions that use both time directions hold for the wave group: unique weak solutions and conservation of the norm, propagation iff reducing, departures from a non-reducing constraint or its orthogonal complement, from `D(L) ∩ K` in one of the two time directions, and forward in time on a graded complex; `D` stands for `D(L)` and `K` for the constraint. The flow of the forward-time reading is the wave group for `t ≥ 0` (the flow of `F.toScheme` is the wave group, `headline_wave`). This is `ApproxFEEC.Verdict`. \[[QB15](#ref-QuennevilleBelair2015)\], Thms 4.6, 4.7, Prop. 4.17; \[[HS12](#ref-HolstStern2012)\], Thm 3.9, Thm 3.10 (elliptic analogues)
